This table shows similar results to those in the Team Rank table, but these are shown on a per-player basis rather than a per-team basis. The description for that table shows the methods to generate these numbers.

When looking at the results, especially in older drafts, many of the results are marked with a *. This indicates that the player has exhausted prospect eligibility and now is being evaluated through an alternative method.

For this alternative method, there is a separate head in the combined neural net model that is predicting a player's MLB WAR by using the bucketing method used in the prospect model. The result shown is the sum of the WAR the player has already accumulated in the majors as well as the expected WAR that the MLB WAR model has for their remaining years of control.

One issue with this current version is that it doesn't actually track their control, rather just using the 6 years after prospect eligibility has been exhausted. This creates issues where individual players can both be overcounted and undercounted. The prospect eligibility metric used often has it exhausting later than it actually does for players who do lose it through service time rather than PA/IP, potentially giving a player an extra year. Conversely, a player who spends considerable time in the minors after losing prospect status could contribute an extra year, or even 2, more than this approach does. While this does lead to some inaccuracies on individual players, on aggregate this balances out.
