For older drafts, this page shows the effects of keeping the last result from the model and propagating it forward forever. For a result that is marked with a *, this represents that the player has stopped getting results from the prospect model—through exhausting rookie eligibility or retirement—which leads to their last result being held in perpetuity.

The current plan to address this is to expand upon the model by projecting future WAR for these players and combining that projection with the WAR that they accumulated to build a more representative system for how well teams are at drafting MLB players, while the current one actually ends up measuring how well teams are at drafting good prospects.
