This table is a collection showing how different teams' drafts evaluate through the pro model, compared to the draft capital that they used to acquire those players.

To determine a team's capital, each pick is assigned an expected WAR for hitters and pitchers. When a team drafts a player, they get the expected capital for the hitter/pitcher that they drafted. This model does not evaluate at which points in the draft should pitchers be drafted to get proper value, simply how well are teams doing with the players vs their expected results.

For each year's value, the team results are the sum of the WAR for the last output for each player that year, unless the player has exhausted prospect eligibility. In that case, it goes through a separate evaluation that takes in their projected MLB WAR in their remaining seasons of team control as well as their already accumulated WAR.

The biggest drawback to these results is that they do not account for trades; if a player was drafted by Team A and traded to Team B, their results count towards Team A, regardless of whether they were traded in their final year of team control or the offseason after the draft.
