This table is a collection showing how different teams' drafts evaluate through the pro model, compared to the draft capital that they used to acquire those players.

To determine a team's capital, each pick is assigned an expected WAR for hitters and pitchers. When a team drafts a player, they get the expected capital for the hitter/pitcher that they drafted. This model does not evaluate at which points in the draft should pitchers be drafted to get proper value, simply how well are teams doing with the players vs their expected results.

For each year's value, the team results are the sum of the WAR for the last output for each player that year, or if a player has exhausted prospect eligibility or retired, it is their final result in the model. This does not reevaluate how well prospects do in the majors, which can lead to some nonsensical results in later years where a team is given credit for drafting a player who becomes a top prospect but then busts in the majors. This is something that is being looked at, but the current model capabilities do not give a better solution.
